# Evidence Policy

## Required metadata
URL/canonical URL, timestamp, rubric/source versions, benchmark dataset version, browser, viewport/device/network profile when applicable, HTML/DOM/headers, screenshots, performance source type, robots/sitemap/schema, console/network errors, accessibility evidence and benchmark references.

## Evidence strength
`SOURCE_VERIFIED` and direct `MEASURED` evidence generally carry highest repeatability when the method is documented. `OBSERVED` can be strong for visual/interaction defects. `INFERRED` must expose its inference and confidence. `NOT_VERIFIED` never becomes a pass.

## Positive evidence
High scores need positive evidence, not only absence of defects.

## Root cause
Findings should distinguish symptom from root cause wherever the evidence supports it.
