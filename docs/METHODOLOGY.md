# Implementation Methodology

## Evidence-first scoring
Scores are outputs of verified subdimensions, not optimistic defaults. Missing evidence is excluded until minimum verified coverage is reached; below that threshold the aggregate is `NOT_VERIFIED`.

## No double punishment
One root cause may affect different lenses where impacts are genuinely distinct, but must not be deducted repeatedly within the same dimension merely because it manifests several times.

## Caps
Caps stop averages from hiding blockers. Every cap is versioned, evidence-triggered and disclosed.

## Confidence
Confidence = 35% evidence coverage + 25% benchmark fit + 20% measurement quality + 20% repeatability. Confidence is about the audit conclusion, not about whether a subjective preference is 'correct'.

## Award vs product quality
Award simulations are separate from SEO, accessibility, engineering, conversion and resilience. The proprietary Digital Excellence Score intentionally does not average award scores to avoid double-counting visual/UX craft.

## Organic visibility
SEO Quality and Organic Visibility are intentionally separate. Search Console/analytics evidence is needed for claims about real organic performance.

## Benchmarking
Benchmark cohorts must be context-compatible and source-governed. Award-version integrity is mandatory: current live sites are not assumed to equal their historical winning versions.

## Calibration
Calibration compares score distributions against recognized and ordinary fixtures/corpus. It should detect score inflation and over-severity, not force every winner into a preselected score.

## Anti-bias
Do not reward complexity, animation, WebGL, dark mode, minimalism or other gallery tropes by default. Evaluate net value and costs.
